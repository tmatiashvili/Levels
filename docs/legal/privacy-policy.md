Levels is made by <developer name>, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what Levels does with your information. The short version: **I don't collect any of your data.**

## What Levels handles

Levels shows the battery of your Apple devices. To do that it uses:

- each device's battery level, charging state and Low Power Mode;
- the name and type of each device and accessory (for example "MacBook Pro" or "AirPods");
- the time of each reading, and up to 7 days of past readings for the history chart.

## Where it is stored

- **On your devices.** Each device keeps this data in its own storage.
- **On your home network.** When your iPhone and Mac are on the same Wi-Fi, they exchange this data directly, encrypted. It doesn't pass through any server.
- **In your iCloud.** If you use iCloud sync, the data is stored in your private iCloud database. Only your devices signed in to your Apple Account can read it. I can't.

The data never reaches me or anyone else.

## What Levels does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third-party code that collects data.
- No location, contacts, photos or other personal data.
- No selling or sharing of data.

## Local network

Levels asks for Local Network access on your iPhone so it can reach the Levels app on your Mac. It uses this only to talk to your own paired devices.

## Purchases

Levels Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and your payment details. Levels checks on your device whether you have Levels Pro; it doesn't send your purchase to me. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Crash reports

If you turn on sharing with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You control this in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements (iPhone) or System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements (Mac).

## Deleting your data

- In Levels: Settings ▸ **Remove all my data** deletes Levels' data from that device and, where it can, from your other devices and your iCloud.
- Deleting the app removes its data from that device.
- You can also delete Levels' iCloud data in your device's iCloud storage settings.

## Children

Levels is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
